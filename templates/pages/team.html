The experience

Good service starts before the appointment does.

Listen first

Understand the outcome, maintenance level and comfort of the client before recommending anything.

Explain clearly

Set realistic expectations around time, upkeep and what a service can actually achieve.

Keep it relaxed

Premium should feel considered and welcoming, not stiff or overly complicated.

Finish with a plan

Leave the client knowing how to maintain the result and when a follow-up would make sense.
